Privacy Policy — shorts-uploader

shorts-uploader is a personal publishing tool used only by its owner.

  • Data we access: only the owner's own account tokens and basic channel information (channel ID, username, follower counts, video view counts, comments on the owner's own videos), obtained through official APIs with the owner's consent.
  • Purpose: to upload the owner's videos, show upload status and statistics to the owner, and let the owner reply to comments on their own videos.
  • Storage: tokens and data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties.
  • Other users: the tool does not collect data about any other person beyond public comments on the owner's own videos, which are shown only to the owner.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time from each platform's app settings; local data is deleted on request.
  • Contact: https://github.com/arabojaeverything-max/arabojaeverything-max.github.io/issues
